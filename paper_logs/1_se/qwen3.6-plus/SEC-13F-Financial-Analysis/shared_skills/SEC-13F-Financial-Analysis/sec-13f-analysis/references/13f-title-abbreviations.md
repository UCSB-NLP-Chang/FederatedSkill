# SEC 13F TITLE Abbreviations

The title column (`TITLE` or `TITLEOFCLASS`) in 13F INFOTABLE filings uses standardized, highly abbreviated codes. Do not expect full English words.

## Equity / Common Stock Indicators
- `COM` - Common Stock
- `SHS` - Shares
- `CL A`, `CL B`, `CL C` - Class A/B/C Shares
- `ORD` - Ordinary Shares
- `COM SHS` - Common Shares
- `COMMON` - Common
- `CAP STK` - Capital Stock
- `SPONSORED ADS` - Sponsored ADRs (treated as equity)
- `TR UNIT` - Trust Units (treated as equity)

## Non-Equity / Derivative / Fixed Income Indicators
- `PFD`, `PRF`, `PREF` - Preferred Stock
- `NOTE`, `DEB`, `BOND` - Debt Instruments
- `ETF` - Exchange Traded Fund
- `RIGHT`, `WARR` - Rights / Warrants
- `ADS` - American Depositary Shares (often treated separately from common stock)

## Classification Rule
A holding is classified as **stock-like** if its title contains at least one equity indicator AND zero non-equity indicators.
